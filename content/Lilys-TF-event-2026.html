Lily's TF event 2026



Event time

The event ends at August 25, 2026 at 7:00 PM UTC. Check the countdown here.


How to participate

Make some TF content using the picture in the post. Once you finish, send it on DeviantArt with the tag LilysTFevent2026 or make a post in #lilys-tf-event-2026 in my Discord server.


Requierements

Human writing

Be creative and write your submission on your own.

Use the picture

It's the picture at the start of this page. It has to remain in GIF format when the submission is finished.

Picture as end result

The picture should be taking place by the end of narration.

Write, don't draw

This is a writing contest. A drawing contest might be done at another time.

About multiple submissions

If you send multiple submissions, only one will be considered for ranking. It should be the best one by my own criteria.


Stuff not allowed for the event

AI writing

Please use your own creativity. You can get inspired, but don't ask a chatbot to write it for you.

Sexual topics

This also disallows sexual related TFs.

Plagarism

Don't copy someone else's idea. If you're gonna do a TF someone else already made, at least make it happen in a different way or under different circumstances.


Rewards

1st place (1+ participants)

I draw your sona/OC in a short GIF similar to the one I made for the event. You're free to suggest what the background can be.

2nd place (4+ participants)

I draw a static picture of your sona/OC with a background. You can suggest what the background will be.

3rd place (8+ participants)

I draw a static picture of your sona/OC with no background.
